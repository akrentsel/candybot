CandyBot

Robots are handing out candy to kids. Come get some.

When
Saturday, October 316 to 8 pm, Halloween night
Where
374 Connecticut AvePotrero Hill, San Francisco. Open in Maps
Free. No sign-up. Everyone welcome.

How it works

  1. Walk up to a robotThey're hard to miss. Costumes encouraged, for you and the robot.
  2. Say "trick or treat"Loud and clear. The robots are friendly but a little hard of hearing.
  3. Hold out your bagThe robot drops in the candy. Say thanks, it likes that.

Good to know

  • Bring a bag or bucket for your candy.
  • Kids should come with a grown-up. Grown-ups can have candy too.
  • Just show up any time between 6 and 8 pm.
