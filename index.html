How it works

  1. Walk up to a robotThey're hard to miss. Costumes encouraged.
  2. Say "trick or treat"Loud and clear. The robots are friendly but a little hard of hearing.
  3. Hold out your bagThe robot hands over the candy. Say thanks, it likes that.

Good to know

  • Free, no sign-up. Just show up any time between 5 and 8 pm.
  • Bring a bag or bucket for your candy.
  • Kids should come with a grown-up. Grown-ups get candy too.

Grown ups welcome!
